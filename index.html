Welcome to LoL Esports Replay Player

Because the native replay player of LoL Esports sucks to me and watching replays on YouTube itself is a spoiler, I decided to make my own LoL Esports replay player.

Start to Watch Replay

How to copy replay URLs without getting spoiler?

  1. Go to LoL Esports and open the replay.
  2. Make sure you are watching the replay on YouTube.
  3. Play the replay and copy the URL by right clicking the YT title.

Features

  • BP and champion status won't get cut off
  • Won't get spoiled because of progress bar
  • Livestream-like immersive experience

User Guide

You can use keyboard to control the player.

  • Space: Play/Pause
  • Arrow Left/Right: Rewind/FF 10 seconds
  • Arrow Up/Down: Volume Up/Down
  • f: FF 5 minutes (useful to skip between games)
  • Shift+f: FF 1 hour (useful to skip between matches)

If the keypress doesn't reponse, click the player by mouse to focus.

Make sure the browser tab of replay player is in full screen mode (like YouTube full screen).
